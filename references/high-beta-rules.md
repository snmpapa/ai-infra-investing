# High-Beta Rules

Use this file for operator names, second-tier AI infrastructure names, and familiar price-zone trades.

## Default Treatment

Treat these as high-beta by default:

- GPU cloud operators
- AI infrastructure operators
- miners pivoting into AI
- data center expansion stories with heavy financing needs
- pure narrative extensions without durable profit visibility

Examples:
- `IREN`
- `CRWV`
- `NBIS`
- sometimes `TSLA` when discussed as an AI or robotaxi story rather than an auto business

## What Makes Them Dangerous

- they can rise faster than fundamentals
- they often depend on financing markets
- customer and contract visibility can change quickly
- the market may confuse operating leverage with durable moat

## Positioning Rules

- Do not treat them as core holdings by default.
- If the user already frames them as trades, keep that framing.
- If included, size them as theme or trading-only positions.
- If the portfolio already has broad AI exposure, recognize that these names are levered expressions of the same theme, not missing exposure.

## When They Deserve a Slot

They may deserve a small slot when:

- the user knows the price zones well
- the user explicitly wants high-beta tactical exposure
- the portfolio has room for a controlled theme sleeve
- there is real evidence of improving contracts, financing, or execution

## What To Say

When discussing these names, explicitly say:

- this is a high-beta operator
- this is not required to complete AI stack exposure
- this is additive upside, not foundational exposure
