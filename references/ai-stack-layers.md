# AI Stack Layers

Use these layers to classify AI-related names before discussing sizing.

## 1. Platform Demand Layer

These companies monetize AI through products, ads, software, cloud usage, or user demand.

Examples:
- `GOOG`
- `META`
- `MSFT`
- `AMZN`

What they earn:
- AI-driven demand
- product monetization
- cloud usage
- enterprise software pull-through

Main risk:
- capex rises faster than monetization

## 2. GPU / Accelerator Layer

These companies supply the main AI compute engines.

Examples:
- `NVDA`
- parts of `AMD`

What they earn:
- training demand
- high-end inference demand
- system platform pricing power

Main risk:
- hyperscaler capex slowdown
- margin compression from competition

## 3. CPU / System Control Layer

These companies benefit from orchestration, inference serving, data handling, and server refresh.

Examples:
- `AMD`
- `ARM`
- sometimes `INTC` as a turnaround case, not a default leader

What they earn:
- server CPU demand
- orchestration and system-level compute
- CPU attach rate in AI infrastructure

Main risk:
- market overstates the speed of CPU profit expansion

## 4. Memory Layer

These companies benefit from HBM, DRAM, NAND, and memory bandwidth constraints.

Examples:
- `MU`
- `DRAM` ETF
- `SNDK`

What they earn:
- AI memory demand
- pricing cycles
- supply tightness

Main risk:
- cycle reversal

## 5. Foundry / Packaging Layer

These companies enable advanced chip production and packaging.

Examples:
- `TSM`

What they earn:
- process leadership
- advanced packaging
- manufacturing bottlenecks

Main risk:
- geopolitical concentration

## 6. Optical / Network Interconnect Layer

These companies help move data between systems and racks.

Examples:
- optical names
- interconnect names
- CPO-related names

What they earn:
- bandwidth demand
- cluster scaling bottlenecks

Main risk:
- market prices future importance before profits fully arrive

## 7. AI Infrastructure Operator Layer

These are GPU cloud operators, AI data center operators, or former miners pivoting into AI infrastructure.

Examples:
- `CRWV`
- `NBIS`
- `IREN`

What they earn:
- GPU rental / cloud utilization
- power and data center execution
- financing and customer contracts

Main risk:
- high capex
- financing dependency
- customer concentration

## 8. AI Payments / Settlement Layer

These are adjacent AI infrastructure names when the user's thesis includes AI agents paying, settling, or transacting.

Examples:
- `CRCL`

What they earn:
- stablecoin rails
- programmable settlement
- agent payment infrastructure

Main risk:
- regulatory path
- rate sensitivity

## 9. Market Activity / Distribution Layer

These companies benefit when AI and crypto narratives increase user participation, trading, or distribution.

Examples:
- `HOOD`

What they earn:
- trading activity
- platform distribution
- retail risk appetite

Main risk:
- activity and sentiment fade
